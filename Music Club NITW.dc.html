01 — About

A room where
everyone plays.

A student-run club backed by the college, with a diverse group of trained artists: vocals, western band, Indian classical and a small production setup, all working out of one jam room. If a stage at NITW has a mic on it, somebody from here has probably stood behind it.

NIT Warangal has grown from its 1959 beginnings as Regional Engineering College into one of India's top technical institutes — and art and culture have always been part of that education. No prior training needed to jam with us.
